        How is this estimated?
        
PromptCarbon analyses your prompt and estimates visible output tokens using Random Forest models. Automatic mode detects word limits with BERT; manual mode uses the limit you enter. Limits are requests, so the actual response may differ.

        
EcoLogits uses the output-token estimate to calculate energy and allocated hardware emissions for a reference Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct scenario: H100 GPUs, batch size 64 and PUE 1.20. This is not a measurement of your computer.

        
SCI = energy × carbon intensity + allocated hardware emissions

        
SCI represents the estimated footprint of one request. The same energy and hardware emissions are used for all six periods; only the Great Britain electricity forecast changes. Operational CO₂ is treated as CO₂e when combined with hardware emissions.

        
Input-processing energy is not explicitly modelled. Reasoning level is shown for information and does not adjust the energy estimate. Forecasts and model estimates are uncertain; savings are not guaranteed.
